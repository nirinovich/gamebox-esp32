# Teaching Scratchpad & User Preferences

## Learner Profile & Context
- **Experience Level**: Absolute beginner to 3D printing and slicing workflows.
- **Hardware**: Original Prusa i3 MK3S with standard 0.4 mm brass nozzle (buse 0.4 mm).
- **Target Project**: 3D-printed enclosure for ESP32-S3 board (tied to the Gamebox console hardware).
- **Target File**: Stated ".SRT file" — resolved to `C:\Users\Nikita\Downloads\ESP32-S3.stl` (337 KB STL 3D mesh).
- **Language / Nomenclature**: Explicit preference for French ("Donne moi un guide en français step by step"). All future lessons, explanations, and guides must be provided in French, while including the English UI equivalents in parentheses for easy matching in PrusaSlicer.

## Teaching Strategy & Focus Areas
- **Teaching Style**: Ultra-granular, click-by-click micro-steps. Zero assumptions about prior software navigation or 3D viewport reflexes. Break each phase into atomic single-action checkboxes. Offer interactive live pair-guidance (Step 1 -> confirm -> Step 2).
- Deliver structured, actionable step-by-step guides in French.
- Emphasize foundational mental models: 3D model (.stl) -> Slicer (PrusaSlicer) -> Machine paths (.gcode) -> Printer (i3 MK3S).
- Prioritize high-impact physical printer safety and bed preparation (IPA wipe, Live Z check) to prevent early failure.
- Demystify PrusaSlicer manipulation: Navigation, "Place on Face" (`F`), Layer height (0.20mm), Perimeters vs Infill, and Organic Supports.
