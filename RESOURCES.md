# 3D Printing with Prusa i3 MK3S & PrusaSlicer Resources

## Knowledge

- [PrusaSlicer Official Documentation](https://help.prusa3d.com/category/prusaslicer_204)
  The definitive guide to manipulating 3D objects, infill, organic supports, and layer heights. Use for: toolpath settings and slicer shortcuts.
- [Original Prusa i3 MK3S Handbook & Guide](https://help.prusa3d.com/category/original-prusa-i3-mk3s_246)
  The official manufacturer manual detailing assembly, electronics, bed maintenance, and safety. Use for: hardware operation and maintenance.
- [Prusa Knowledge Base: First Layer Calibration (Live Z)](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/first-layer-calibration-i3_112364)
  Visual reference for diagnosing first-layer extrusion height. Use for: getting perfect bed adhesion without nozzle digging.
- [Prusa Knowledge Base: Flexible PEI Steel Sheets Guide](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/flexible-steel-sheets_2195)
  Cleaning protocols (90%+ Isopropyl Alcohol) and bed surface types. Use for: cleaning and preventing part detachment or bed damage.
- [Prusa Research: Organic Supports in Depth](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/organic-supports_476722)
  Technique for non-destructive tree-like supports. Use for: bridging port cutouts and overhangs in electronic enclosures.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/prusa3d (Reddit)](https://reddit.com/r/prusa3d)
  Active community of thousands of Prusa owners. Use for: real-world print failure diagnosis and slicer profile reviews.
- [Prusa3D Official Community Forum](https://forum.prusa3d.com/)
  Direct interaction with Prusa engineers and experienced 3D makers. Use for: hardware issues, firmware updates, and calibration tips.
- [Printables by Prusa](https://www.printables.com/)
  Official curated 3D model repository with user-verified pre-sliced G-codes and comments. Use for: testing proven models and finding alternative ESP32 mounts.
