0.4mm Nozzle ("Buse") Rules of Thumb

Parameter Value Range ESP32 Enclosure Target Engineering Rationale
Nozzle Diameter 0.40 mm 0.40 mm brass Standard factory E3D V6 nozzle; optimal speed/detail ratio.
Extrusion Width 0.42 – 0.45 mm 0.45 mm default Plastic squishes slightly wider than orifice for inter-track bonding.
Min Layer Height 0.05 mm 0.15 mm (fine detail) Excessively thin layers cause heat creep and long print times.
Standard Layer Height 0.15 – 0.20 mm 0.20 mm QUALITY Ideal balance of dimensional accuracy for snap-fits and speed.
Max Layer Height 0.30 mm (75% limit) 0.30 mm (rough draft) Exceeding 75-80% nozzle diameter prevents layer squish and causes delamination.

Hardware Pre-Flight Checklist (i3 MK3S)

  1. Clean the Smooth PEI Bed: Wipe cold sheet thoroughly with a paper towel and 90%+ Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA). Never touch the print area with bare fingers (grease kills adhesion).
  2. Inspect Nozzle Tip: Ensure no cold hardened plastic glob hangs from the 0.4mm nozzle before mesh bed leveling runs.
  3. Insert SD Card: Export G-code from PrusaSlicer to the SD card, eject safely, and insert into the printer's side slot.
  4. Select & Print: Rotate the silver click wheel to Print from SD and click your file.
  5. Audit Layer 1 (Live Z): Watch the first layer go down. Extruded lines must be smooth, flat, and connected without gaps (too high) or gouging the bed (too low).
