# Issue 07: Game-Specific Procedural Visual Atmospheres

Status: closed

## Description
Overhaul the procedural Canvas 2D rendering routines across all 7 games: Connect Four gravity drop with elastic bounce, Tron neon bloom trails with headlights, Pong phosphor comet trails with speed heat, Battleship 360° sonar sweep, Dominoes Havana emerald felt table with 3D ivory tiles, Snake viper eyes and glowing food core, and Tic-Tac-Toe laser strokes.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Connect Four animates falling chips with gravity acceleration and elastic bounce.
2. Tron light cycles render bright core lines with neon bloom and crash shockwaves.
3. Pong renders comet motion trails behind the ball and sparks on paddle contact.
4. Battleship renders a continuous clockwise sonar sweep and splash/fire effects.
5. Dominoes renders an emerald felt radial gradient and 3D ivory tiles with soft drop shadows and pulsing playable end halos.
6. Snake draws directional robotic eyes on the leading segment and a pulsing glowing apple core.
7. Tic-Tac-Toe draws strokes dynamically and renders a laser beam across the winning three marks.
